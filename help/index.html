Guides for novelists and worldbuilders.

How to build a world, outline a book, and write it. Every guide here is also inside Penpoint.

New to Penpoint

Start here.

Create your first book Name it, pick a template, and you are writing. Penpoint ships with a demo book if you would rather poke around first. Build your world Characters, locations, and items live beside the manuscript, not in a separate app you forget to open. Outline your chapters Plan the shape of the book on a corkboard or an outline, and reorder it as the story changes. Write the prose Manuscript Mode is the writing side. Your worldbuilding stays one panel away while you draft. Export when you are ready Word, PDF, EPUB, Markdown, or plain text. Your work is yours, and it leaves whenever you want.
