Book Pages: Front and Back Matter

Book Pages are front and back matter: the pages around your story. Before Chapter 1: Copyright, Dedication, and Epigraph (a short quote that opens the book). After the last chapter: Cast of Characters, Glossary, Acknowledgments, About the Author, Also By (a list of your other books), and Review request (a note asking readers for a review).

Where to Find Them

In Manuscript Mode, export the whole book as Word, EPUB, PDF, Rich Text, Markdown, or Plain Text, and a Book Pages step follows Formatting & Preview. A single chapter or scene, or Standard Submission Format (a manuscript, not a book), skips it.

Note: Scrivener exports skip Book Pages; add those pages in Scrivener.

Choosing Pages

  • Check a page to print it. In a PDF these pages are unnumbered and Chapter 1 is page 1. Only a PDF shows a live preview; other formats include the checked pages too.
  • Copyright comes with standard text. A blank page shows Write instead of Edit, and its checkbox stays dimmed until it has text.
  • ISBN (the number that identifies a published book; you may not have one) and Copyright year belong to this book. An empty year box prints this year; checking Copyright fills an empty box with it.

Shared Pages and This Book’s Own

Copyright, About the Author, Also By, and the Review request are written once and shared by all your books. Dedication, Epigraph, and Acknowledgments belong to one book.

  • Customize for this book appears once the shared page has text and copies it into this book, leaving your other books alone.
  • Use shared version appears on this book’s copy when there is a shared version to go back to. It erases this book’s text (no undo) and leaves the shared page alone. The first click turns the button red and asks “Really?”; click again to confirm, or it resets after four seconds.

Writing a Page

Click Edit beside a page. The editor matches your export’s paper size and font, with bold, italic, center, and link. A link takes a web address (http or https) or an email address, typed as is; anything else is refused with a message. Dedications and epigraphs start centered.

Penpoint saves as you type. Esc and Back both return to the list.

Type a marker anywhere in a page and it fills in at export:

  • {title}: the Title box under Title Page in Formatting & Preview, or your book’s title if blank.
  • {author}: the Author box there. Both boxes show when Include title page is checked.
  • {year} and {isbn}: the Copyright year and ISBN boxes on the Book Pages step.

A marker with nothing to fill in removes its whole paragraph (or Shift+Enter line): “ISBN: {isbn}” with no ISBN drops that line. Copyright’s standard text works this way too, so fill in Author to keep its copyright line.

Tip: A page holds up to 60 paragraphs, fewer if long. At the limit the editor says This page is full and stops taking more, so nothing is cut off.

Cast of Characters and Glossary

These two pages draw on your Worldbuilding but print only names and the one line you write for each, never profile or description text, so a spoiler can’t slip in. Both start empty; anything you add later stays off until checked.

Pick who appears (Cast) lists your characters; Pick terms (Glossary), your items, places, and terms. Until the page has something to print (on the Glossary, a checked entry with a line), its row says Pick first; then it counts what is checked, like “3 picked,” and the button says Edit picks. The page checks itself the first time it has something to print; uncheck to leave it out.

  • Check a name to include it. Unchecking (or None) keeps the line you typed. A renamed character prints under the current name; a deleted one drops off, line included.
  • Add main cast (Cast only) checks every character whose Importance is Primary, set on the character’s page. Everyone and None check or uncheck every visible name.
  • Type a line after a checked name; the page prints the name in bold, then the line, like Elara Voss: Navigator of the Wren. On the Cast a line is optional.
  • Plain A to Z (Cast only). Off, the Cast runs Primary, Secondary, Background, each group A to Z. On, everyone is A to Z. The Glossary is always A to Z.
  • Glossary terms. Type your own terms, such as a festival, under Add your own term and press Enter (or Add term). An entry without a line stays in your list, says “Needs a line to print,” and is left off the page. The X beside a term you typed deletes it and its line at once, no confirmation; unchecking keeps both.

Tip: Each page holds up to 150 entries, fewer if the lines are long. At the limit, checking stops until you uncheck an entry or shorten a line.

Where Your Pages Live

What you write for one book (pages, shared-page copies, Cast and Glossary picks, ISBN, year) is saved inside the book file, so it travels in a .penpoint file. Shared pages live outside it, with your Penpoint settings on this computer. If you sync your settings with Penpoint Cloud, your other computers get them too; otherwise a book you move arrives without them, so copy their text over by hand.

In a Series, pages are per Book: each Book keeps its own ISBN, year, picks, book-only pages, and shared-page copies, and its Cast and Glossary list only its own characters, items, and places.
