Exporting Manuscripts

Save your manuscript as a file to send, print, or open elsewhere.

Note: Exporting never changes or deletes your manuscript; it only makes a copy.

Opening the Export Window

Click Export (an upward arrow) at the top of Manuscript Mode, or choose Export from the three-dots icon (⋮) at the top-right in a narrow window. Settings, under Import/Export, has an Export Manuscript button too.

Choosing What to Export

  • From the full manuscript: straight to a list of chapters.
  • From inside a chapter or scene: choose the scene, chapter, or whole manuscript first; a scene or chapter skips the list.

Selecting Chapters

Pick chapters to include (all start selected), with word counts and a running total. In a Series (several books grouped in Penpoint), only the Book you're in is exported (switch Books for another); its title page title starts as that Book's own name and the subtitle as "Book N of [your series name]" (N is the Book's place in the series).

Choosing a Format

  • Word Document (.docx): opens in Word, Google Docs, and book formatting apps. No pictures.
  • EPUB: the e-book format for Apple Books and most e-readers. No pictures.
  • PDF: fixed pages laid out like a printed book. Justified paragraphs stay justified, with even word spacing, and only they get hyphens at line ends. A very long word or web address wraps instead of running off the page. Pictures print centered, shrunk to fit if needed, with captions.
  • Markdown: plain text with formatting kept as simple symbols, like *italic* and **bold**. Pictures go in a folder beside the file; keep the two together.
  • Plain Text: no formatting (italics and bold are dropped) or pictures; works with any text editor.
  • Rich Text (.rtf): for older word processors, or when a publisher or contest asks for .rtf files. No pictures.
  • Exporting to Scrivener: a Scrivener 3 project folder; chapters become folders, scenes become documents. Pictures stay in Penpoint.
  • Standard Submission Format: a Word file in the layout agents and magazines expect: double spacing and a running header. No pictures.

Tip: Not sure? Word is the safe pick. Book has pictures? Choose PDF or Markdown.

Note: OpenDyslexic is a typeface some readers with dyslexia find easier. With Use OpenDyslexic Font on (Settings, Interface, Accessibility), a PDF prints in it. Word, Rich Text, and EPUB only ask for it, so it shows only where OpenDyslexic is installed. Word and Rich Text ask only while Font is set to Match my writing font. Sending Word to an agent? Set Font to Times New Roman or switch OpenDyslexic off.

Note: Exporting the whole book as Word, EPUB, PDF, Rich Text, Markdown, or Plain Text? A Book Pages step can add a copyright page, dedication, About the Author, and more; only pages you check are added. See Book Pages.

Formatting Options

A Scrivener Project export skips this section: Scrivener's Compile sets fonts, spacing, the title page, and layout. Your paragraphs keep their first-line indents.

Title Page

Check Include title page (Shunn short-story styles have no separate title page; see below), then fill in:

  • Title: defaults to your book's name.
  • Subtitle: optional. Turning off Print subtitle on title page keeps what you typed.
  • Author: the name printed under the title. It starts as the name you gave during setup, if any; changes apply to all your books.
  • Show word count: rounded to the nearest 1,000 (Shunn styles: nearest 100, or nearest 500 from 17,500 words, novella length).
  • Contact Info: optional, for submissions. A Contact name (your real name, if you write under a pen name) and a Contact block for address, phone, or email. If Contact name or Author is blank, Penpoint uses the other. Penpoint remembers these; clear them to remove them.

The title page prints the title, subtitle, and author in every format except Scrivener; EPUB leaves out contact details and the word count.

Standard Submission Format adds two choices. Style is Standard (novel), Shunn Modern (short story) in Times New Roman, or Shunn Classic (short story) in Courier New; both Shunn styles follow William Shunn's short-story manuscript format and make Page One, with contact details upper left and word count upper right. Identify me as is Full details, Email only (just the first email address in your contact block), or Anonymous (no name or contact details on the title page, in the running header, or in the file properties).

Running Header

A running header is the line at the top of each page with your name, title, and page number. Add a running header prints it on every page but the title page (Shunn stories start on page two). If blank, Header title and Your surname use your title page's title and author name.

Only Word and Standard Submission Format have one. A PDF numbers pages at the bottom center instead, skipping the title page and any Book Pages (Chapter 1 is page 1).

Chapter Headers

Include chapter headers adds a heading at each chapter's start, in three parts: Include "Chapter N" numbers, Include chapter titles (off leaves a bare "Chapter 1"), and Include chapter subtitles.

Scene Breaks

Include scene breaks offers five separators:

  • Spaced asterisks: * * *
  • Standard manuscript: #
  • Compact asterisks: ***
  • Horizontal line: ---
  • Blank line only: just extra space

Note: "Standard manuscript" here and under Page Setup is a setting, not Standard Submission Format.

Typography

  • Font: your writing font is the Editor Font under Settings, Manuscript Mode (Use Global Font means your Interface font).
    • Word, Rich Text, and Standard Submission Format: your writing font, Times New Roman, Georgia, or Courier New.
    • PDF and EPUB: always your writing font.
    • Markdown and Plain Text: none; the opening app decides.
  • Line spacing: Single, 1.5 lines, or Double (Word, PDF, Rich Text, and Standard Submission Format).
  • Paragraph style: Indented (book style) or Spaced (digital).

Page Setup

Word, PDF, Rich Text, and Standard Submission Format exports have a Paper size: Letter (8.5" x 11", the US standard), A4 (international standard), or Trade (6" x 9", common print book trim size). Expand Margins & Indent to set margins; a PDF needs a bottom margin of at least 0.42 inch to show page numbers. If Paragraph style is Indented, set First-line indent to Compact (0.25") or Standard manuscript (0.5").

Note: The preview on the right shows a PDF's real pages. If Penpoint can't draw them, it shows a simpler sample; your saved PDF isn't affected. Other formats get a close approximation (Scrivener shows its folder structure), so the file may differ slightly in the app that opens it. Before sending a file, especially for a submission, open it in your actual program (Word, Google Docs, and so on) and confirm it looks right.

Reset formatting to defaults, at the bottom of the settings, resets every setting and show/hide switch for the format you're on, keeping what you've typed and your paper size.

Reviewing and Exporting

Click Export (labeled with your format, like "Export DOCX") to save the file. Your save window asks before overwriting. Then click Show in Folder (Open Folder for Scrivener) or Done to close the Export window.

Tip: Penpoint remembers each format's formatting choices; only what you're exporting (scope and chapter selection) starts fresh each time. Signed into Penpoint Cloud (the optional paid service that backs up and syncs your books)? Formatting choices, author name, and contact details reach your other computers too; Paper size stays separate on each computer.

Note: Submitting by email or an online form instead of attaching a file? See Pasting Your Manuscript Into a Form.
