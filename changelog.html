1.2.0

May 2, 2026

Added

  • The Chapters Page, Reimagined - One of Penpoint's most-requested features is finally here. The Chapters page has been rebuilt from the ground up. Each chapter now has its own customizable block-based layout — just like Character Sheets — so you can design a chapter view that fits how you plan stories. Every scene now stands on its own with its own characters, locations, items, and POV (tags and arcs stay at the chapter level, where they belong). The new outliner sidebar mirrors Manuscript Mode, so navigating between chapters and scenes feels instantly familiar. Configure your default scene layout in Settings → Scene Layout. Your existing chapters carry over with their current view intact — nothing to redo.
  • Build Your Own World's Calendar - Your book now has its own calendar system. Pick Standard (Gregorian) for real-world stories, Day Counter for stories told by day number ("Day 47" instead of "March 12"), or go wild with Custom and design a fantasy calendar from scratch: your own months, weekdays, festival periods, leap days, even BCE/CE-style epochs. Every scene and chapter date uses your chosen mode automatically. This also paves the way for a FUTURE rework of the Timeline view.
  • Customize Your Character Arc View - The Character Arc page now has a gear icon in the visualizer header to control what's visible. Choose to show plot points for all characters, just the active one, or none at all. Toggle "Always show labels" off for a clean comparison view — you can still hover or click any keyframe to reveal its label. The active character's line is also a bit thicker now so it stands out from any overlays. Plus: you can now assign chapters (and specific scenes) to individual plot points on character arcs, just like story arcs. Open any plot point in edit mode to link chapters, or switch to view mode to see them as a read-only list. Linked chapters also show up in the compact list view on the arc block itself.
  • Customize Your Book Covers - Your books on the shelf just got a lot more personal. Pick from 12 preset colors or mix your own with a built-in color picker. Layer on a texture like Grain, Linen, Leather, or Parchment for that hardcover feel. Or go all out and upload your own cover image, cropped to fit. If your image already has the title on it, you can hide Penpoint's text overlay so it doesn't compete. All your choices travel with your book through backups and restores.
  • Search Works in Manuscript Mode — The Glorious Searchbar popup (Ctrl+K) now works while you're writing, not just in Worldbuilding. Search for a character, location, item, chapter, or anything else — clicking a result opens it in your Minipen sidebar without taking you away from your manuscript. Scene results jump you straight to that scene in the editor.
  • See Where Your Locations Appear - Every location now shows which chapters it appears in, right on its detail page. Open "The Haunted Library" and see at a glance that it shows up in Chapters 3, 7, and 12. In edit mode, you can assign or remove chapter connections directly from the location itself.
  • Version Protection for Your Books - If you open a book on one computer with a newer version of Penpoint, then try to open it on another running an older version, Penpoint will tell you exactly what's happening and ask you to update before opening the book.
  • New "Alice" Theme - A soft cherry blossom pink theme for those who like their writing space a little more lovely. Find it in Settings → Interface alongside the other themes.
  • Reset the Demo Book - The Sherlock Holmes demo book now has a Reset option in its right-click menu. Restore it to its original state any time you want a clean tour.

Improved

  • Find Characters by Their Nickname - Anywhere you add a character to something (a scene, a relationship, an item, a location, a chapter, or an arc), you can now search by their alias instead of their full name. Type "Lizzy" and Elizabeth Bennet shows up.
  • Filter Items by Location - You can now filter your Items list by location. Select one or more locations in the filter sidebar and only items tied to those locations will show. Works the same as your other filters.
  • Smoother Startup Experience - If you haven't opened Penpoint in a while, you might have a few things waiting: an update, a data upgrade, maybe an announcement. They now queue up and appear one at a time in a sensible order, so you're never overwhelmed by overlapping pop-ups. The analytics prompt also has a new "Not now" option.
  • Refreshed Chapters Tutorial - The Chapters tutorial has been rewritten to walk you through the new block-based layout, with clearer steps and better example data.
  • Tag Viewer for Multi-Scene Chapters - The Tag Viewer now works properly across chapters with multiple scenes, showing tags at the right level so you can see what's tagged where without confusion.
  • Keyboard Navigation in Notebook and Items - Arrow keys now move to the visually adjacent card instead of jumping alphabetically, and Alt+Left/Alt+Right cleanly goes back or forward without also shifting your highlight. The Items page also picked up Up and Down arrow keys for jumping between rows.
  • Every Word Counts - The community word count in your stats panel now shows every word written in Penpoint since launch, right down to the last one. We just crossed a million, and watching that number climb is a lot more fun when you can see it tick upward.

Fixed

  • Chapter Descriptions Preserved Across Upgrades - Fixed an upgrade path where chapter descriptions could be lost when migrating to the new block-based chapter layout. Every upgrade path now preserves your chapter descriptions completely.
  • Character Arc Overlays Lining Up - When comparing multiple character arcs, plot point markers on background arcs could drift out of alignment with the active character's, even when both arcs had the same number of points. They line up properly now.
  • Crosslinks Now Show Scenes - Items, Locations, and Characters that appear in chapters now drill down to specific scenes, not just the chapter as a whole. Character chapter lists also show the correct scene numbers (instead of internal positions), and Story Arc plot points correctly filter to their assigned scenes in view mode.
  • Arc Scene Assignment Clarity - Assigned scenes in your arc plot points now show a filled square so you can see at a glance what's linked. The Assign and Remove buttons are always visible, no hovering required.
  • Right-Click Submenus Staying on Screen - Fixed several cases where right-click submenus could get cut off at the screen edge, especially near a corner. When a submenu flips to the opposite side to fit, it now sits flush against the parent menu instead of overlapping or leaving a gap.
  • Cleaner Scene Names - Adding a second scene to a chapter no longer leaves you with two rows labeled "Empty Scene". New scenes are now named "Scene 1", "Scene 2", and so on. Scenes you've already named are left alone.
  • Duplicate Scenes Under Rapid Clicking - Fixed a rare race condition where rapid clicks during scene creation could occasionally create duplicate or orphaned scenes. Scene creation is now reliable even under heavy clicking.
  • Calendar and Chapter Card Polish - Several calendar UI improvements and chapter card display fixes, plus better submenu positioning throughout.
  • Notebook Rename Fixes - Fixed two rename issues: renaming a folder in the sidebar could fail if that same folder was visible in the main grid at the same time (the rename box would flash and disappear before you could type), and renaming a note while it was open in the viewer didn't refresh the title inside the editor until you switched notes.
  • Export Title Page Remembers Your Book - The Title field in the manuscript export dialog now always shows your current book's name, not whatever you last exported. If you've customized the title before, Penpoint remembers your custom version per book.
  • Location Renames Now Update the List - Renaming a location in edit mode didn't update the name in the left-hand list until you reloaded the app. Now the list updates on its own right after you stop typing, and the title field feels snappier too.
  • Faster Search Results After Editing - If you fixed a word in your manuscript and then searched for it, the old version could still show up in search results for several seconds. Now results update the moment you open the searchbar.
  • Manuscript Cursor Hiding Behind Toolbar — The typing cursor could scroll up behind the formatting toolbar, letting you type text you couldn't see. Fixed.
  • Domain URLs in Manuscripts - Fixed a rare bug where domain endings like .it could sneak into your manuscript as hidden links. Your text is now safe from sneaky URLs.
  • Cutting Text No Longer Reduces Your Word Count — Using cut and paste to move text around no longer affects your daily word count.
  • Word Count Stats After Midnight — If you were writing past midnight, the "Manuscript size (+X today)" line in your daily stats could show the wrong number. Fixed.
  • Manuscript Folders Staying Put - If you made a few empty folders in Manuscript Mode's chapter outliner back to back and dragged them into order, they could quietly swap positions when you came back later. Now they stay exactly where you put them.
  • Notebook Panel Widths Drifting — If you resized the folder tree or preview panel in the Notebook and then switched to another page and came back, the panels would shift slightly each time. Fixed.
  • Back Button Not Clearing Item Selection - On the Items page, pressing Back right after selecting an item didn't actually deselect it, which threw off the back and forward history. Fixed.
  • Search Result Highlights on Hover — When hovering over a search result, the matched search term could become hard to read. Fixed.
  • Closing the App During the Welcome Tour - The X button now properly closes Penpoint during the first-run welcome tour. Before, you'd have to wait for the tour to finish (or force-quit) to get out.
  • Historical / Non-fiction Template - Renamed the "Historical" New Book template to "Historical / Non-fiction" for better discoverability.

1.1.2

March 30, 2026

Added

  • Chapter Drafts — Scene Drafts now work for chapters that haven't been split into multiple scenes. Right-click any single-scene chapter in the outline and you'll find "Chapter Drafts" — save a snapshot, view old versions, or restore a previous draft, same as always. If you later split the chapter into scenes, your saved drafts carry over automatically. Nothing is lost.
  • Find & Replace with Formatting — The Find & Replace bar now has a collapsible "Advanced" section at the bottom. Use Match to narrow your search to only bold, italic, or underlined text — perfect for tracking down foreign words or specific styled passages. Use Apply to add formatting to your replacements, like swapping a nickname for an italicized foreign name in one operation. Works with Replace All and "Replace in rest of manuscript" too.
  • Whole Word Matching in Find & Replace — A new toggle in Find & Replace lets you match whole words only — so searching for "Yes" won't accidentally catch "Yesterday" or "Yes-terday". It's on by default. Penpoint remembers all your Find & Replace settings between sessions, so everything's exactly where you left it next time you open it.
  • Find & Replace Button in the Toolbar — There's now a magnifying glass button in the manuscript toolbar that opens and closes Find & Replace. It stays highlighted while the panel is open, so you always know at a glance whether it's active.

Fixed

  • Notebook Display Fixes — Fixed the note editor panel overflowing off-screen when the navigation sidebar was pinned, and fixed note titles in list view squishing to a few characters per line at high display scaling.
  • Flowstate No Longer Forces Fullscreen — Starting a Flowstate session used to automatically push you into fullscreen mode, and ending it would pull you back out. Now the two are independent — run Flowstate in your normal view, or combine it with fullscreen. Your call.
  • Find & Replace Stays in Sync When Switching Chapters — If you had Find & Replace open and switched to a different chapter or scene, the results would still show matches from the old chapter. Now it automatically re-searches the new content and clears any previous replace counts.
  • Smart Quotes After Punctuation — Typing a quote mark after a period or other punctuation could produce a straight quote instead of a curly one. Fixed.
  • Curly Quotes After Em Dashes — Quote marks after an em dash could face the wrong direction when ending interrupted dialogue. Fixed.
  • Minipen Notes Sorting — Fixed sorting in the Minipen Notes sidebar: numbered folders now sort naturally (1, 2, 10 instead of 1, 10, 2), changing the sort order updates the list immediately, and the sort menu now matches the layout used elsewhere in the app.

1.1.1

March 27, 2026

Added

  • Honoring Penpoint's First Purchase — The About page now recognizes Jendriel as Penpoint's Founding Supporter — the very first person to buy a license. Thank you!
  • Zen Mode for Manuscripts — Press F11 or click "Fullscreen Mode" in the footer to fade away the toolbar, sidebars, and header over a smooth 2.5-second transition — just you and your words. Hover the screen edges to slide out the chapter outline or Minipen as overlays, or hover the top to drop down the formatting toolbar on demand. New Settings options under Fullscreen Mode let it activate automatically when you start typing and exit after 30 seconds idle — Flowstate also triggers it.
  • The Worldbuilding Toolbar Guide — A new help article walks you through every icon and button in the toolbar — the save indicator, writing stats, help menu, announcements bell, clock, and more. Find it in the User Guide under "The Worldbuilding Toolbar."

Improved

  • Restoring Backups Got a Big Upgrade — Backup history is now accessible from three places: the Book Selector, Settings > Storage & Backups, and the "Saved" dropdown in the toolbar. When you restore, you choose how: Overwrite Current Book (recommended — replaces your book in place) or Restore as New Book (creates a separate copy so nothing gets replaced). Either way, Penpoint automatically creates a safety backup first, so you can always undo a restore if you change your mind.
  • Total Word Count in the Footer — The word count bar at the bottom of Manuscript Mode now shows your current scope alongside your total manuscript word count — so you can see "1,234 words (50,000 total)" at a glance without opening the stats panel. Click the word count to hide the total if you prefer a cleaner look. Penpoint remembers your preference.
  • Right-Click Anywhere in Your Outline — You can now right-click on empty space in the Manuscript outline to quickly add a new chapter or group — no need to reach for the + button.
  • Notebook Folder Drag-and-Drop — Improved drag-and-drop for folders in the Notebook — you can now drag folders between levels, out of subfolders, and into new positions with cleaner visual feedback.
  • Faster Note Creation in the Notebook — Creating a new note now drops your cursor right into the title field so you can start typing immediately. Press Tab to jump straight into the writing area — no clicking required.
  • User Guide on Smaller Screens — The Help Guide is now much more readable on smaller screens and narrower windows. The article index adjusts automatically so your help articles always have room to breathe.

Fixed

  • Dragging Notes & Folders to the Binder Deleted Them — Dragging a note or folder from the main area into the Binder (the root level of your Notebook) would make it vanish. Fixed.
  • Undo in the Notebook — Pressing Ctrl+Z in a note when there was nothing left to undo could replace your entire note with whatever was in the previous note you had open. Fixed — undo now only applies to changes you made in the current note.
  • Item Titles Overwriting Each Other — If you edited an item's title and quickly clicked another item before the save finished, the first item's new title would get written onto the second item instead. Fixed — saves now always go to the right item, no matter how fast you click.
  • Character-Item Relationship Notes Disappearing — If you typed a note describing how a character relates to an item and switched to view mode too quickly, the note would vanish. Fixed — it now always saves before the switch.
  • Editing a Trait Option Now Updates Your Characters — If you fixed a typo in a trait option (like correcting "Bule" to "Blue"), any character already assigned that value would be stuck showing the old one — and you couldn't clear it without a workaround. Now renaming a trait option updates all your characters automatically.
  • Typing on macOS Sequoia — macOS Sequoia's autocorrect could silently rewrite words while you typed, causing cursor jumps and random duplicate text. Penpoint now disables system-level autocorrect within the editor to prevent this. Your smart quotes and dash settings still work exactly as before.
  • @Mention Fixes — Fixed several @mention bugs: Escape now closes the suggestion popup without deleting what you typed, custom item types now show their real names instead of ID numbers, the Home key no longer jumps to the wrong line when a line starts with a mention, and punctuation right after a mention link (like a comma) no longer orphans to the next line. You can also now press Tab to confirm a suggestion.
  • Items Uneditable After Filtering by Tags — If you filtered your Items list by specific tags and then created a new item, the item would immediately vanish from view and couldn't be edited — Ctrl+E did nothing, and the Edit button was unresponsive. Fixed — the detail panel now stays open so you can fill in the item's details before it shows up in your filtered view.
  • Minipen Search Now Takes You to the Right Spot — Clicking a scene result in the Minipen search would open the right scene but drop you at the top instead of at the text you searched for. It now jumps straight to your match and highlights it.
  • Daily Word Count Now Reflects Real Progress — Deleted words now subtract from your daily word count. If you rewrote a sentence, it no longer counts the original words and the rewrite — you see the actual net change for the day. This means your word count can go negative if you spent the day cutting, which is an honest look at a heavy editing session.
  • Unnumbered Chapters Showing Wrong Numbers in @Mentions — If you marked a chapter as unnumbered (a prologue, interlude, or epilogue), @mentioning it would still display it as "Chapter 4: Prologue" or similar. It now shows just the chapter title, and numbered chapters that follow show the correct sequential number.
  • Writing Stats Resetting at Midnight — If you write past midnight, your daily word count delta (the "+350 today" next to your manuscript size) would silently disappear until you restarted the app. It now resets automatically at midnight like it should.
  • Writing Streak Showing Wrong Numbers — Your writing streak could get stuck showing the wrong count after opening the app. Fixed the underlying cause and made the streak calculation more resilient.
  • Character List Scrolling Back Into Position — When you returned to your character list after visiting another page, Penpoint would scroll through the whole list to get back to where you were instead of jumping straight there. Fixed — it now snaps immediately to your last selected character.
  • Item Type Dropdown Not Updating — Changing an item's type while in edit mode would tick the right checkbox but the label wouldn't update until you left edit mode. Fixed — it updates immediately.
  • Empty Folders Now Stay Where You Put Them — Dragging an empty folder to a new spot in your outline would snap it right back the moment you let go. Fixed — empty folders now stay wherever you drop them.
  • Smart Quotes & Backspace — When smart quotes were on, backspace would first convert a curly quote back to a straight quote before deleting it — requiring two presses instead of one. Fixed.
  • Relationships Block at Half-Width — Fixed relationship entries spreading apart with large gaps when the block is placed side-by-side with another block.
  • Writing Stats Panel Getting Cut Off — On some screens, the writing stats dropdown could extend past the edge of the window, clipping the text and button. Fixed.
  • Notebook Text Overflowing After Paste — Pasting text into a Notebook note could cause content to run off the right edge of the screen instead of wrapping properly. Fixed.

1.1.0

March 20, 2026

Added

  • Right-Click Menus on Chapters, Items & Locations — Chapters, Items, and Locations now all have full right-click menus. Right-click a chapter card to open it, jump to the Manuscript editor, reorder, insert a new chapter before or after, or delete. Right-click items or locations to add, delete, change importance, sort, and more. Detail panels have their own right-click menus too — quick access to edit mode, profile image, type changes, and other common actions without hunting through the UI.
  • Writing Stats: Manuscript Size & Last Writing Day — Your analytics dropdown (the chart icon in the toolbar) now shows your current manuscript word count with today's change — great for tracking editing progress toward a target — plus a quick look at how your last writing day went, so picking up after a break you can see at a glance what you accomplished last time you sat down.
  • Sort Locations Ascending or Descending — The Locations sort menu now includes Ascending and Descending options, so you can flip any sort method — alphabetical Z to A, newest first, or reverse type order.
  • Credit Where Credit Is Due — Check out Settings → About for a little something new. You know who you are.

Fixed

  • Opening a Chapter in Manuscript Mode Now Goes to the Right Chapter — The first time you opened a chapter in Manuscript from the Chapters page, it would load whichever chapter you had open last instead of the one you clicked. Second click would work. Fixed — it goes to the right chapter every time now.
  • Multi-Value Traits Lost on Import — If a character had a trait with multiple values selected (e.g., "Brave" and "Trustworthy"), importing that character would collapse them into a single combined value instead of two separate ones. Fixed.
  • Typing Glitch on Mac — On some Macs, the cursor could suddenly jump to the start of a sentence and drag text along with it while you were typing. Fixed.
  • Character List Expanding to Full Screen — When you clicked the arrow to expand your character list to fill the whole screen, it would stay in a single column instead of spreading out across the space. Fixed — it now fills the width with as many columns as will fit.
  • Deleted Items Staying on Screen — Deleting an item would remove its card from the list, but the detail panel would stay open showing the deleted item as if it were still there. Now the panel closes cleanly when an item is deleted.
  • Spellcheck Flagging Possessives of Character Names — Penpoint auto-whitelists your characters, locations, and items in the spellchecker — but writing "Charlye's" (possessive) would still get flagged as a spelling error even though "Charlye" was fine. Fixed.

It's now available alongside Bold, Italic, Underline, and Highlight.   • Scene Drafts Preserved After Versioning — Fixed scene drafts going missing after creating a manuscript version. Both features now stay completely independent.

  • Spellcheck Underlines Hidden in View Mode — Spellcheck squiggles now only appear when you're actively editing. In view mode, they stay out of the way.
  • Novel View No Longer Indents Centered Text — The "Novel" paragraph setting adds a first-line indent to your prose, but was also indenting centered and right-aligned text. Those are now left alone.
  • Tags Section Card Previews — Entity cards in the Tags section could show raw code for cleared fields, and @mentions in descriptions were dropped entirely. Both fixed.
  • Discord Status Stuck on "Worldbuilding Mode" — Opening Penpoint directly into Manuscript Mode would show "Worldbuilding Mode" in your Discord status until you navigated somewhere. Your status now reflects where you actually are from the moment you open the app.
  • Manuscript Tutorial Freezing at Step 3 — On high-resolution or custom-scaled displays, the Manuscript Tutorial could get stuck at Step 3 when selecting a chapter. Fixed.
  • Character List Scrolling on Return — Navigating back to the Characters section would trigger a scroll animation down to your selected character. Now it jumps there instantly.
  • Notebook Back Button Now Returns to Root — The back button in the Notebook now treats the root level as a proper stop in navigation history, so you can always get back to where you started.
  • Tutorial Polish — Thanks to great feedback from CovOp and Tapgiles on Discord, we've fixed a variety of bugs across many tutorial walkthroughs.
  • Items Tutorial Restart Not Appearing — The "Restart Tutorial" option was missing from the Help menu on the Items page. Fixed.
  • Manuscript Toolbar Button Spacing — Two toolbar buttons could end up jammed together at certain window widths. Fixed.
  • Notebook Right-Click Menu Positioning — Right-clicking near screen edges could cause the context menu to flicker into place or appear far from your cursor. Fixed.

1.0.23

March 15, 2026

Added

  • Import a Manuscript When Creating a Book — When creating a new book, you can now choose "Import a Manuscript" instead of starting from scratch. Pick your file, preview and customize your chapters, optionally scan the whole manuscript for characters, locations, and items, then name your book and go. Everything — your prose, your chapters, and any discovered story elements — lands in your new book ready to work with.
  • Half-Width Blocks for Every Block Type — You can now place any block side-by-side on your character sheet layout — not just Traits and Numbers. Tags next to a Character Arc. Appearance paragraph next to Age. Whatever makes sense for your characters. Click the new split-column icon in a block's toolbar to toggle it half-width, and click again to expand it back.
  • Spellcheck in Worldbuilding (Beta) — Spellcheck now works in your character, location, and item notes — not just in Manuscript Mode. Right-click an underlined word for suggestions or to add it to your dictionary, same as always.
  • Character Page Right-Click Menus — Right-click anywhere on the Characters page for quick access to common actions. Right-click a character in the list to add, delete, or change sort/view options. Right-click a character's detail panel to toggle edit mode, change importance, swap the profile image, jump to the Relationship Map, edit their sheet layout, and more — all without hunting through menus.
  • Manuscript Right-Click Menu — Undo, Redo, Select All, and Paste without Formatting are now in the right-click context menu. Paste also now correctly preserves bold, italic, and other formatting.
  • Smarter Help Guide Search — The search bar in the Help Guide now searches the full content of every article — not just titles. Find what you're looking for even if you only remember a word or phrase from inside the article. Results show the article name, the section where the match was found, and a highlighted snippet so you can tell at a glance if it's the right one.
  • Revamped Tutorials — Every interactive tutorial across Penpoint has been overhauled. Tooltips now point exactly where they should, example data is more realistic, and the Notebook page gets its own full walkthrough for the first time. No more stuck tooltips or empty panels during the tour.
  • Expanded New User Walkthrough — The new author experience has been polished and expanded with new slides, smoother animations, and better guidance so you feel right at home from the start.
  • Manuscript Import & Export in Settings — You can now import and export your manuscript prose directly from the Import & Export settings page — no need to open Manuscript Mode first. The page is also reorganized into Worldbuilding and Manuscript sections, so everything has its place.
  • Em Dash Shortcut — Three dashes (---) now make an em dash automatically.

Fixed

  • Content Scanner Crash on Older Macs — Fixed a crash that prevented the content scanner from running on macOS Monterey (12.x) and earlier.
  • Manuscript Search Now Actually Works — Searching for text from your manuscript wasn't showing any results, even when the text was right there. Fixed. The Minipen sidebar search also now shows a preview of the matching passage so you can confirm it's the right scene before clicking.
  • Custom Dictionary Words Not Sticking — Adding a word to your custom dictionary could appear to work (it showed up in the list) but the red underline wouldn't go away.
