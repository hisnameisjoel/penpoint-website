Before you start

Questions writers ask.

Planning

“How do I plan a whole novel in a month?”

Lay the story out on the Corkboard, one index card per chapter, and drag the cards until the order feels right. Every chapter holds its own scenes, so October's outline becomes November's list of what to write next.

Your cast

“How do I keep my cast and timeline straight?”

Every person in the story gets their own page in Characters, and the Timeline shows who appears in which chapter. While you write, the Minipen sidebar keeps that whole world open beside the manuscript.

Word count

“Can I really hit 1,667 words a day?”

Click the bar chart icon at the top of the screen and Writing Stats shows the words you've added to your manuscript today. Fifty thousand words over thirty days is 1,667 a day, so you always know where today stands.

Week two

“What do I do when I hit the week-two wall?”

Fullscreen Mode hides everything but your words, and Writing Stats keeps your writing streak in view. The plan you built on the Corkboard is still there, telling you which scene comes next.

Safety

“What if I lose my draft?”

Penpoint saves as you write and makes automatic backups you can restore from. From November 1, Penpoint Cloud adds an encrypted backup off your computer, free all month.

What now

“The November challenge is still on. What do I use now?”

Penpoint gives you Manuscript Mode for the draft, worldbuilding pages for everything around it, and a daily word count to pace the month. It is free through November, with no account and no credit card.

Installing

Quick setup guide.

  1. Run the downloaded .exe installer.
  2. Follow the installation prompts. Penpoint will launch when finished.

Penpoint won't open? Check or repair Microsoft WebView2.

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg file.
  2. Drag Penpoint to your Applications folder.
  3. Open Penpoint from Applications. It will launch normally.

.AppImage (recommended: works on any distro and updates itself)

  1. Make the AppImage executable. Open a terminal and run:
    chmod +x Penpoint_*.AppImage
  2. Double-click the .AppImage file to launch Penpoint, or run it from the terminal:
    ./Penpoint_*.AppImage

.deb package (Ubuntu, Debian, Pop!_OS, Mint). Installs through your package manager, but you update it manually each release; the AppImage updates itself.

  1. Double-click the downloaded .deb file. Your package manager will handle the rest.
  2. Find Penpoint in your app launcher and open it.
