Private by design

Only you can read it. Not even Penpoint.

Your password locks your books. Before a book leaves your computer, it's encrypted (scrambled so it's unreadable) with a password you choose. That password never leaves your computer, so Penpoint can store your books but can't open them.

Your Recovery Kit is the spare key. When you set up Cloud, you save 12 words somewhere safe, like a password manager or a printout. Forget your password and those 12 words get you back in.

The books on your computer are always yours. If you ever lose both your password and your Recovery Kit, nobody can unlock the cloud copy, but nothing on your computer changes. How the encryption works

Questions

Common questions

Do I need Penpoint Cloud to use Penpoint?

No. Penpoint is a one-time purchase and works completely offline, with no account. Penpoint Cloud is an optional subscription that adds an encrypted cloud copy of your books, sync between computers, and Minipen, the Penpoint phone app.

How much does Penpoint Cloud cost?

Penpoint Backup is $5 a month or $45 a year. Penpoint Sync is $10 a month or $96 a year and includes everything in Backup, up to 5 computers kept in step, and the Minipen phone app. Both plans have unlimited storage. Tax is added at checkout where it applies.

Is there a storage limit on Penpoint Cloud?

No. Both plans have unlimited storage, so you can back up every book you write, with every image and every earlier draft, and never watch a meter or move up a plan to make room. The one rule is fair use: the Terms let us slow uploads on an account past 15 GB, with at least 7 days' notice, and nothing already in the cloud is touched.

Can Penpoint read my books in the cloud?

No. Your books are encrypted on your computer with a password only you know, before anything is uploaded. Penpoint stores them but cannot read them. More on the password and Recovery Kit

What happens to my books if I cancel Penpoint Cloud?

The books on your computer are the originals and nothing happens to them. Cloud keeps working until the end of the time you paid for. After that the cloud copy is kept for 30 days, then deleted, with warning emails first.

Changed your mind before your paid time runs out? A Resume button in Settings > Penpoint Cloud switches you straight back on.

What if I forget my Penpoint Cloud password?

Use your Recovery Kit, the 12 words you save when you set up Cloud, to choose a new password. If you lose both the password and the Recovery Kit and no computer is still signed in, nobody can unlock the cloud copy. The books on your computer are not affected.

Which phones does Minipen run on?

Minipen, the Penpoint phone app, is on the App Store for iPhone (iOS 16.2 or later) and on Google Play for Android phones (Android 7.0 or later). It needs a Penpoint Sync plan, and you set up your Cloud account in Penpoint on your computer first.

Does Penpoint Cloud work offline?

Yes. Penpoint always saves to your computer first, so you can keep writing with no connection. Cloud catches up on its own when you're back online.
