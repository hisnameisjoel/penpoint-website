What Is Penpoint?

Penpoint is a desktop app where fiction writers write their manuscript and organize their entire fictional world in one place.

Writers juggle Word for drafting, spreadsheets for characters, Notion for worldbuilding, and a whiteboard for timelines. Every time they need to check a detail, they leave their writing and lose their train of thought. Penpoint puts the manuscript editor, character profiles, relationship maps, arcs, locations, and search all in one place.

Standout Features

Chapter Scanner

Import a manuscript from Word, EPUB, or plain text. Right-click a chapter and scan it. Penpoint reads the prose and finds every character, location, and item mentioned. Review the matches, click Apply, and the worldbuilding side populates in seconds. This is usually the "oh wow" moment.

Glorious Searchbar

Hit Ctrl+K (or Cmd+K) and search across your manuscript, characters, locations, and notes at once. Find any detail in your entire book instantly without leaving what you're working on.

Manuscript Editor

Clean editor with scene management, chapter groups, drafts and versions, and a 60-language offline spellchecker. Import from Word, EPUB, Markdown, or plain text. Export to any of those plus PDF and RTF.

Visual Worldbuilding

Relationship maps, story arc visualizers, character fortune curves, nested locations, timelines, and tags. Customizable character profiles with blocks you add or remove. Everything is cross-referenced and searchable.

Flowstate

A forward-only writing mode where sentences lock behind you as you write. Timed sprints with alternating writing and editing windows. Designed for writers who keep rewriting instead of moving forward.

Privacy-First

All data stored locally on the writer's computer. No account, no cloud, no AI. Works completely offline. Auto-backup writes to the user's own cloud storage folder for safety.
