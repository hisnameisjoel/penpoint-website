Support

Need a hand? Here's how to get help.

Help for the Penpoint desktop app, Penpoint Cloud, and Minipen, the Penpoint phone app.

Before contacting support, check the Help Guide. Lots of questions already have a good answer there.

Built into Penpoint

Contact Support, right from the app.

Report a bug, share an idea, or ask a question without leaving Penpoint. Click the ? at the top right, then Contact Support.

Available in Penpoint v1.3.6 and later. On an earlier version, Discord and email below work just as well.

  1. 1Pick a topicBug Report, Feature Idea, Question / Need Help, Penpoint Cloud Issue, and a few more.
  2. 2Add picturesA screenshot of Penpoint is already attached. Add up to four images, or remove any you don't want to send.
  3. 3Write it outIn your own words. Close the form and what you typed is still there when you come back.
  4. 4Choose the extrasInclude technical logs if you like, and tick whether you'd like a reply by email or Discord.

Nothing leaves your computer until you press Send. Open See what's included to read the system report word for word before it goes. After sending, you get a reference number like Pen-1042. What gets sent, and how long it's kept.

Penpoint hit an error? The error screen has a Send to Penpoint button that opens the same form with the error already filled in.

Get in touch

Start on Discord. It's the quickest way.

Questions usually get answered fastest on the Penpoint Discord, and other writers there can often help too. Email works just as well for anything private.

Email

 Write in.

Best for anything private, like your account or billing. A real person reads every message.

support@penpoint.app

Writing from Minipen? Open Settings, then About, and under Diagnostics tap Share Logs. Email the file or attach it to your Discord post. It holds app activity only, never your book's text.

A great report says:

  • Which app (the desktop app or Minipen)
  • Which device (Windows, Mac, Linux, iPhone, or Android)
  • What you did, and what happened instead
  • A screenshot, if you can
