What Penpoint Is

Penpoint is a desktop app where fiction writers draft their manuscript and organize their entire fictional world in one place.

Writers end up juggling Word for drafting, a spreadsheet for characters, Notion for worldbuilding, and a whiteboard for timelines. Every time they need to check a detail, they leave their writing and lose the thread. Penpoint puts the manuscript editor, character profiles, relationship maps, arcs, locations, timelines and search in one window.

It has no generative AI of its own and no grammar engine, which is part of why ProWritingAid is such a natural fit. Penpoint is where the words get written and the story gets kept straight. It was never meant to be the editing layer on top.

A Quick Tour

Manuscript Editor

A clean editor with scene management, chapter groups, drafts and versions, and a 60-language offline spellchecker. Import from Word, EPUB, RTF, Markdown or plain text, and export to any of those plus PDF. This is the surface Desktop Everywhere would attach to.

Chapter Scanner

Import a manuscript, right-click a chapter and scan it. Penpoint reads the prose and finds every character, location and item mentioned. Review the matches, click Apply, and the worldbuilding side fills itself in. This is usually the moment people sit up.

Visual Worldbuilding

Relationship maps, story arc visualizers, character fortune curves, nested locations, timelines and tags. Character profiles are built from blocks the writer adds or removes. Everything is cross-referenced and searchable.

Flowstate

A forward-only writing mode where sentences lock behind you as you write, with timed sprints that alternate writing and editing windows. Built for writers who keep rewriting their first paragraph instead of reaching the second.

Search Everything

Ctrl+K or Cmd+K searches the manuscript, characters, locations and notes at once. Writers find any detail in the whole book without leaving the page they're on.

Local and Offline

Everything is stored on the writer's own computer. No account required, works with no internet connection, and auto-backup writes to a folder the writer chooses. Their book is theirs.

The Integration Request

Everything below is written for whoever owns the Desktop Everywhere app list. Forward this section, or the whole page, as-is.

The ask

Add Penpoint to the Desktop Everywhere supported-app list on macOS and Windows.

  • App Penpoint (penpoint.app)
  • macOS bundle ID com.penpoint.app
  • Windows executable penpoint.exe
  • Built with Tauri v2 (WKWebView on macOS, WebView2 on Windows)
  • Contact support@penpoint.app

Why I think this is a list entry, not an engineering problem

  1. Accessibility is clean. Penpoint's editor exposes a standard WebKit AXTextArea with full text-marker attributes, the same surface TextEdit presents. Grammarly Desktop attaches to it with no special handling on either side.
  2. A ProWritingAid subscriber traced it on his own Mac (Everywhere 2.07, build 825). He found com.penpoint.app present in the client's bundled app list, but absent from the integration list the app downloads at runtime. In that same session Scrivener and Slack attached normally, while no AppContext was ever created for Penpoint and nothing was logged.
  3. Being a webview app isn't the blocker. The published list already includes several Chromium-based apps on both platforms.

What I can do to help

  • The download at the top of this page is the full app, so anyone on the team can install it and test against a real editor right now. Nothing to ask me for first.
  • Run any diagnostic you want on Windows or macOS and send you the output.
  • Ship a build with whatever instrumentation or accessibility change would make this easier. I'm one person, so turnaround is days, not quarters.

I'm not asking about Linux, since I know Desktop Everywhere doesn't support it.
