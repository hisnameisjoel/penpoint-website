Installation Guide

Penpoint isn't code-signed yet, so your OS will show a security warning. This is normal for beta software — here's how to get past it.

  1. Your browser may warn that the file "isn't commonly downloaded." This is normal for new software — click "Keep" or "See more → Keep anyway" to save the file.
  2. Run the downloaded .exe installer.
  3. Windows Defender SmartScreen will show "Windows protected your PC."
  4. Click "More info" (the small text link below the warning).
  5. Click "Run anyway".
  6. The installer will complete and Penpoint will launch.

  2. Open the downloaded .dmg file and drag Penpoint to your Applications folder.
  3. When you first open Penpoint, macOS will show "Penpoint can't be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software." — click "OK" to dismiss.
  4. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  5. Scroll down — you'll see a message about Penpoint being blocked. Click "Open Anyway".
  6. macOS will ask one more time — click "Open" to confirm. Penpoint will launch normally from now on.

  2. Make the AppImage executable. Open a terminal and run:

  3. Double-click the .AppImage file to launch Penpoint, or run it from the terminal:
    ./Penpoint_*.AppImage

A .deb package is also available on our GitHub releases page for Debian/Ubuntu users.

What We're Looking For

You don't need to test everything. Pick the areas that matter to your writing workflow and give us your honest take.

  • Onboarding What's the first-run experience like? Could you figure out how to get started, or did you get stuck?
  • Search Does the Glorious Searchbar actually help you find things fast? Try it with characters, locations, items, notes.
  • Characters & Relationships Is the relationship tracking and map useful, or is it overkill for what you need?
  • Export & Import Does exporting to PDF, Word, or Markdown work for your workflow? Try importing too.
  • Stress Test It If you're working on something with enough moving parts that you've lost track of details, throw it at Penpoint. Try to break it.
