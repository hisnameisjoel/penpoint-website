Installation Guide

Penpoint isn't code-signed yet, so your OS will show a security warning. This is normal for beta software — here's how to get past it.

  1. Your browser may warn that the file "isn't commonly downloaded." This is normal for new software — click "Keep" or "See more → Keep anyway" to save the file.
  2. Run the downloaded .exe installer.
  3. Windows Defender SmartScreen will show "Windows protected your PC."
  4. Click "More info" (the small text link below the warning).
  5. Click "Run anyway".
  6. The installer will complete and Penpoint will launch.

  2. Open the downloaded .dmg file and drag Penpoint to your Applications folder.
  3. When you first open Penpoint, macOS will show "Penpoint can't be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software." — click "OK" to dismiss.

  5. Scroll down — you'll see a message about Penpoint being blocked. Click "Open Anyway".

What We're Looking For

You don't need to test everything. Pick the areas that matter to your writing workflow and give us your honest take.

  • Manuscript Mode Write your prose directly in Penpoint. Try creating scenes, splitting and merging them, reordering chapters, and switching between writing and worldbuilding. Does it feel natural?
  • Manuscript Import Bring an existing manuscript from Word, EPUB, Markdown, or plain text. Does Penpoint detect your chapters correctly? Try the Chapter Scanner to auto-connect your worldbuilding.
  • Flowstate Start a timed writing sprint with Ctrl+Shift+F. Your existing text locks behind you — you can only write forward. Does it help you push through?
  • Story & Character Arcs Visualize your plot as layered curves with keyframes. Track a character's fortune as rises and falls. Overlay multiple arcs to compare. Do these tools reveal anything useful about your story?
  • Everything Else Relationships, search, tags, locations, export — put it all through its paces. If you're working on something complex enough that you've lost track of details, throw it at Penpoint. Try to break it.
